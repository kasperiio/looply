Circular running and cycling routes from your door

Looply builds loop routes that start and finish in the same place. Drop a pin or search an address, choose how far you want to go, and it returns up to ten circular routes back to where you started — with an elevation profile, a surface breakdown, and a GPX file for your watch or head unit. Free, and no account needed.

What you can ask for

  • A distance — anything from a 1 km shakeout to a 100 km ride.
  • A surface — stick to asphalt, mix it up, or seek out dirt, gravel and footpaths.
  • Well-lit streets — for running after dark, Looply prefers roads and paths that are actually lit.
  • Flat or hilly — bias the route toward climbing or away from it.
  • Road, gravel or MTB — cycling routes match the bike you are on.

Why loops

Out-and-back routes are half the scenery for the same effort, and an A-to-B route leaves you stranded. Looply scores every candidate on how much ground it retraces, so what you get back is a genuine loop rather than a line folded in half.
